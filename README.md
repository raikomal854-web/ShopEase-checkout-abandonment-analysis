# ShopEase — E-commerce Checkout Abandonment Analysis

### Business Analysis Mini Project | E-commerce Case Study

---

## 📌 Project Overview

ShopEase is a fictional e-commerce business experiencing customer drop-off during the checkout process.

This Business Analysis case study focuses on understanding the problem, identifying stakeholders, exploring potential causes, defining the right questions to investigate, evaluating possible solutions, and establishing measurable success criteria.

The project demonstrates a structured BA approach to moving from a **business problem to a potential solution and measurable outcome**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

ShopEase is experiencing a high level of customer abandonment during checkout.

Customers add products to their carts and initiate the checkout process but leave before completing their purchases.

This can result in:

- Lost sales opportunities
- Reduced revenue
- Lower checkout conversion
- Poor customer experience
- Inefficient use of customer acquisition efforts

### Business Objective

Identify potential factors contributing to checkout abandonment and develop a structured approach to improving checkout completion and the overall customer experience.

---

# 🔎 Business Analysis Approach

The analysis follows a structured six-stage approach:

**Business Problem**
↓
**Stakeholder Analysis**
↓
**Potential Causes**
↓
**Business Questions**
↓
**Possible Solutions**
↓
**Success Metrics**

This approach helps ensure that potential solutions are connected to the underlying business problem and can be evaluated through measurable outcomes.

- Problem Statement
- Business Impact
- Project Purpose

- Key Business Question

## 2. Stakeholder Analysis

Identified key stakeholders involved in or affected by the checkout problem and analyzed their:

- Role
- Interest
- Influence
- Information they can provide
- Engagement approach

A **Power × Interest Matrix** was also used to categorize stakeholders and determine an appropriate engagement strategy.

📊 **Deliverable:**  
`ShopEase-Stakeholder Analysis (1).xlsx`

---

## 3. Possible Causes Analysis

Identified potential factors that could contribute to checkout abandonment.

Rather than treating assumptions as confirmed facts, potential causes were framed as areas requiring further investigation and evidence.

Examples include:

- Unexpected shipping costs
- Checkout complexity
- Account registration requirements
- Payment failures
- Limited payment options
- Website performance
- Customer trust concerns
- Delivery expectations

📊 **Deliverable:**  
`ShopEase — Possible Causes Analysis.xlsx`

---

## 4. Business Questions

Developed targeted questions to investigate the problem and understand where and why customers may be dropping out of the checkout journey.

The questions focus on areas such as:

- Checkout drop-off points
- Payment performance
- Shipping costs
- Registration requirements
- Device-specific behaviour
- Customer complaints
- Checkout experience

📊 **Deliverable:**  
`ShopEase — BA Questions.xlsx`

---

## 5. Possible Solutions

Developed potential solution areas based on the identified problem areas.

The solutions are intended to address customer friction and improve the checkout experience while remaining aligned with the business objective.

📊 **Deliverable:**  
`ShopEase — Possible Solutions.xlsx`

---

## 6. Success Metrics

Defined measurable indicators that can be used to evaluate whether checkout improvements are successful.

Key metrics include:

- Checkout Abandonment Rate
- Checkout Conversion Rate
- Payment Success Rate
- Checkout Completion Time
- Checkout-related Customer Complaints

# 📈 Expected Business Outcome

The analysis provides a structured foundation for ShopEase to:

- Understand potential checkout friction
- Identify areas requiring further investigation
- Prioritize stakeholder input
- Evaluate possible improvement opportunities
- Define measurable indicators of success

The next logical stage would be to validate the identified hypotheses using actual customer, product, and transaction data before implementing any solution.

---

# 💡 Key BA Takeaway

A key principle demonstrated through this project is:

> **Do not jump directly from a business problem to a solution.**

A structured BA approach involves:

**Understand the problem → Identify stakeholders → Investigate potential causes → Ask the right questions → Evaluate solutions → Define success**
