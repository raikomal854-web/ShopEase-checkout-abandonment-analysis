# ShopEase — E-commerce Checkout Abandonment Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This is a Business Analysis portfolio project based on a fictional e-commerce company, ShopEase.

The project focuses on understanding why customers may abandon the checkout process before completing their purchases.

## 🎯 Business Problem

ShopEase is experiencing a high level of customer abandonment during the checkout process, potentially resulting in lost sales opportunities and reduced revenue.

## 🔍 Analysis Objectives

- Understand the business problem
- Identify key stakeholders
- Analyze stakeholder influence and interest
- Identify potential causes of checkout abandonment
- Define questions and data requirements
- Develop potential solutions
- Recommend measurable improvements
